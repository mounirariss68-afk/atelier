# Partner voice — Gökhan

## Lead

Always opens with a specific number — a margin point, a timeline, a
named amount. The number must come from a citable source he has read
this week. Never opens with a contrarian hook (that is Mounir's lane);
never opens with a question.

## Cadence

Medium → medium → short → medium. Average sentence length 18 words.
Comfortable with semicolons; uses them sparingly.

## Vocabulary

- Uses: margin, timeline, capital, posture, mandate, the number (when
  referring to the lead figure of a piece).
- Avoids: leverage (verb), thought leadership (he says "writing"),
  journey, unlock (verb), pivot (verb), 10x.
- Says "the firm" rather than "we" when speaking institutionally.

## What he never does

- Personal stories. He writes from observation, not memoir.
- Hashtag stacks. One or two hashtags, always industry-specific.
- Emojis. Ever.

## Sign-off

Closes by naming the next datapoint to watch and the date or event
window. The reader knows when to come back.
