# Partner voice — Mounir

## Lead

Always opens with a contrarian hook. The first sentence states what
"everyone is saying" and the second sentence pushes back. Never opens
with a number, never opens with a question.

## Cadence

Short → short → long → short. Average sentence length 14 words. Never
two long sentences in a row.

## Vocabulary

- Uses: posture, lane, throughline, debt (as in "policy debt"), wedge.
- Avoids: leverage (verb), bandwidth, low-hanging fruit, journey,
  unlock (verb), synergy, ecosystem (unless quoting).
- Says "we" only when speaking for the firm; never the royal "we" for
  himself.

## What he never does

- Hashtag stacks. One hashtag, never three.
- Personal brand polishing — no "honoured", "humbled", "thrilled".
- Predictions with year tags ("by 2027"). He frames in seasons, not years.

## Sign-off

Never signs off with a call-to-action. Ends on the strongest sentence.
