ere's a focused UI design review of the opportunity page based on your screenshot.

What's not working
1. Three-zone color split feels chaotic
The page reads as three disconnected bands: dark navy header → cream body → dark navy "Account snapshot" card. The eye doesn't know where to settle, and the cream middle band looks like a different product than the dark surfaces around it. Your other Atelier surfaces are dark-on-dark; this one breaks that system.

2. Action bar is doing too many jobs at once
"Draft POV / Build proposal / Capture meeting / Research account / Prototype / 5C Qualifier" are all rendered as equal-weight pills in one row. In reality these are very different actions (creative drafting vs. data capture vs. a qualifier checklist). The orange highlight on "Draft POV" is the only hierarchy signal, and "5C Qualifier" floats off to the right looking like a stray.

3. The mock-fallback warning is shouting and ignored
The "All agent runs and human decisions are logged… mock-fallback (Claude unavailable)…" line is long, low-contrast amber-on-cream, all-mono, and stretches across the full width. It's an important provenance disclaimer but it currently reads as decorative chrome that users will tune out.

4. Status row buries the money
WON · PROB: 100% · $1400k — the deal value is the most important number on the page and it has the least visual weight. The two pills steal the eye away from the dollar figure.

5. Tabs underline is too quiet
The active "Brief" tab has a thin gold underline that disappears against the cream background, and inactive tabs have nearly the same color as active. There's no clear "you are here."

6. "AMBER — VERIFY · 5/13/2026, 1:37:10 AM · Re-run Scout"
The amber dot is meaningful (run status), the timestamp is forensic, and "Re-run Scout" is a primary action — but they're laid out as if they were the same thing. The amber chip floats next to ISO-ish timestamp text with no card boundary, and the action sits orphaned on the right.

7. Header right rail wastes the dark canvas
Partner: Mounir Ariss / Lead: Layla Haddad / Next: Mobilize phase-2 charter — these are key facts but they're set as small mono inline text, not as a structured "people/state" block. The dark header has tons of room for a much clearer ownership panel.

Suggested fixes (in order of impact)
Pick one surface tone for the page body. Either keep it dark (matches the rest of Atelier and lets the gold accents sing) or commit to a light theme — but don't sandwich light between two dark zones. Easiest win: drop the cream and let the body inherit the dark bg-deep you already use elsewhere.
Split the action bar into two groups.
Primary work ("Draft POV", "Build proposal", "Capture meeting", "Prototype") as a card-anchored toolbar near the tabs.
References / scoring ("Research account", "5C Qualifier") as a smaller secondary row, or move "5C Qualifier" into the Brief tab where it belongs.
Promote $1400k to a hero stat. Render it as a large gold figure with a small "Deal size · 100% probability · WON" caption underneath. Demote WON / PROB chips to that caption line.
Re-treat the mock-fallback line as an actual banner.
Two lines max.
Sentence case, not all caps.
Use the existing amber MonoChip + a single explanatory sentence: Mock fallback active — Claude unavailable. Outputs are synthetic and not evidence.
Optional: collapse to a small pill ("⚠ mock fallback") with a tooltip once the user dismisses it once.
Make tab state unambiguous. Active tab in text-text + 2px gold underline; inactive tabs in text-text-mute with no underline. A single hover state in between.
Wrap the run-status row in its own card. Something like:
Left: amber chip + "Pursuit Scout · last run 5/13 1:37 AM · awaiting verification"
Right: "Re-run Scout" + "Verify"
This turns three orphaned elements into one scannable status panel.
Build a proper header ownership panel. On the dark header right side, render Partner / Lead / Next as a stacked block with subtle dividers and the people's initials as small avatars. Same data, but it becomes a glanceable "who owns this and what's next" rather than three lines of mono text.
Account snapshot card should drop its navy fill once the body goes dark — it'll naturally distinguish itself with a 1px border and slight elevation, rather than competing with the header for "I'm the dark thing."
Want me to implement any of these? I'd suggest starting with #1 (unify the surface tone) and #4 (fix the mock-fallback banner) — those two changes alone will make the page feel coherent.
