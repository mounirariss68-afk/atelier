## 2. Partnership thesis
Cognirise wants to **represent established consulting/technology brands in MENAT** — leveraging their references and know-how, while giving them a low-risk regional foothold. **Primary selection criterion: fit of service offering and industry coverage with cognirise.** Brand nationality is NOT a constraint (US, Turkish, Polish, Indian all acceptable) — referenceable enterprise track record is what matters.

## 3. Candidate assessment (5 companies researched)
- **Argano** (US; ~3,000 staff; PE-backed roll-up, 29 acquisitions) — **LEAD "represent in MENAT" target.** AI practice (ArganAI, agent orchestration, AI governance), data & enterprise-tech, top-tier Oracle/SAP/Microsoft/Salesforce partner. Industries match GCC demand (mfg, FS, life sciences, E&C, telecom). Minimal MENAT presence = additive, no conflict. ArgaNext is their corp-dev vehicle. Watch-outs: ERP-implementation DNA could pull cognirise into being a platform channel; acquirer reflex.
- **Thoughtworks** (~10–12k, Apax-owned) — **platform partner, NOT competitor.** Their AI/works™ platform fills cognirise's platform gap for clients that require one. Engage gladly under a non-compete covering cognirise-introduced accounts.
- **BGTS / Bilge Adam** (Turkish; 2,000+ engineers; HSBC, ING, Standard Chartered, Vodafone, Mercedes-Benz refs; Temenos depth) — **preferred engineering backbone / delivery partner.** Nearshore economics power cognirise's pricing wedge. Not AI-native (so no AI-differentiation conflict).
- **Mobven** (Istanbul; Payten subsidiary; ~170 staff; fintech mobile/wallet/onboarding) — niche **studio product subcontractor** for fintech builds.
- **Prometa.ai** (Istanbul AI boutique) — **competitor, monitor only** (near-duplicate offering, thinner references).

**Portfolio recommendation:** two-tier structure — Argano (premium reference brand) + BGTS (scaled delivery backbone), with Thoughtworks as an on-demand platform layer. These are complementary, not competing.
