Tasama Agentic BPO — Point of View

Context

Tasama operates a back-office BPO for ~40 KSA government bodies. Volume is growing 18% YoY; the cost-to-serve is structurally rising because the talent pool for case-handling is shrinking faster than throughput can absorb. [Tasama FY25 ops review, internal]

Hypothesis

~62% of repetitive case classifications and 41% of evidence-collection steps are agentifiable inside 90 days, with partner-grade quality controls held by the human supervisor pool.

Evidence

  • Process discovery across 6 service lines (Q1 2026 walkthrough).
  • Two BPM diagrams annotated for agentifiability (Mermaid).
  • Comparable benchmark: DGE Effortless agent build pattern.

Proposal

90-day MVP: 3 named agents (Triage, Evidence Collector, Quality Sentinel) wrapped in The Atelier shell. Senior delivery led by AICo partner Mounir Ariss. No bench-warming.

Value case

~$1.4M run-rate cost-to-serve compression in year-one; ~3x throughput per supervisor on agentified queues.

Next steps

  1. Mobilize charter and partner-final read.
  2. Stand up the 90-day MVP scope on day-3 deliverable.
  3. Anchor the value case to the audit log from week one.
