---
name: Partner opportunity tag semantics
description: Why external partner tags reuse co-sell involvement but do not imply economics or portal access.
---

Treat an external partner tag as involvement, not a negotiated revenue split, an internal owner assignment, or an access grant. Keep it on the canonical co-sell association rather than introduce a separate tagging model.

**Why:** users need to tag firms early, before commercial terms or access decisions exist. The older co-sell form's 50/50 starting values are form defaults, not a business rule; copying them into tagging would silently invent terms.

**How to apply:** new tagging surfaces must preserve retained terms, use neutral unset economics for new links, and never provision partner portal access. External-sharing reports must omit other partner identities and all commercial split/internal-note fields.
