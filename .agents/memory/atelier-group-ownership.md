---
name: Atelier group ownership economics
description: Durable modeling and approval rules for group ownership, commercial rights, and partner distributions.
---

Legal ownership, voting/control, and profit participation are independent effective-dated facts. Sponsor revenue shares, royalties, and franchise terms are commercial rights, never cap-table interests. Ownership holders link through stable person/entity records rather than display names. A structure's lifecycle status (planned, incorporated, franchise-operated, archived) is separate from its maker-checker approval status.

**Why:** Legal title can differ from economic participation, as in sponsor structures, and future entity splits or transfers must preserve prior structures without turning commercial arrangements into equity.

**How to apply:** Add dated versions rather than overwriting. Reject overlaps, cycles, and totals above 100%. Permit missing commercial terms only when explicitly pending. Economic changes and distribution approvals require a different approver from the proposer and transactional revalidation under row locks. Distribution approval atomically freezes the exact approved profit-right snapshot and remains outside payroll.
