---
name: Riser route conformance
description: C16 evidence boundary between route inventories, approved migration divergences, and real route-assistant equivalence.
---

A complete route inventory with no unexplained annotations is only a migration baseline. It must not be reported as route/assistant equivalence when executor contracts are absent or behavioral dimensions remain unproven.

**Why:** A generated inventory can mechanically approve every route-only surface and produce “zero unexplained” while proving no shared authority, validation, scope, side-effect, confirmation, idempotency, audit, navigation, or error behavior. This makes the gate vacuous.

**How to apply:** Discover every mounted user-facing route tree, not only Atelier routes. Keep non-migrated families explicitly divergent with route-specific owner, reason, review date, and rollback. A migrated family needs one shared executor/service contract and behavioral probes for every applicable C16 dimension; mark read-only dimensions N/A explicitly rather than “proven.” Do not advance C16 while all executor contracts are null.
